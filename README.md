# Social Media Impact on Student Life

Analysis of how students' social media use relates to their sleep, stress, mental health and GPA, with an interactive Power BI dashboard.

[Open the live dashboard (Power BI)](https://app.powerbi.com/view?r=eyJrIjoiYTc0MGU2MDItMTBlYS00OWFjLTkxZDgtMGUyODZmMGVjODMxIiwidCI6ImQxOTkxYzRjLThjNDUtNGFhNy05ZWNmLTUyYWVmODhhMzAyZCIsImMiOjEwfQ%3D%3D)

## Dataset

Social-media-impact-on-life.csv: 4,500 students, one row per student.

## Tools

SQL (PostgreSQL), Excel (Pivot Table), Power BI

## Key Findings

- More daily use goes with less sleep: average sleep falls from 8.0 hours (0-2 hours of use) to 4.1 hours (14 hours of use).
- Stress rises with usage: the average stress score goes from 6 to 30 across the same groups.
- GPA falls with usage: from 3.81 to 2.54.
- 81.8% of students report a beneficial overall impact, 14.5% neutral and 3.7% negative.
- Platforms look similar: average daily use is 5.1-5.5 hours on every platform.
- Sleep is almost the same across academic levels and device types (6.7-6.9 hours).

These are relationships, not proof of cause. Groups with very high usage are small (for example, 55 students at 14 hours), so their averages are less reliable.
